# RM001 Lock Rules

The RM001 Restaurant Master Hero is locked as the official architecture reference.

Do not change:

- Camera perspective
- Central corridor
- Bar position
- Kitchen position
- Dining area
- Ceiling language
- Material palette
- Lighting philosophy
- Premium dinner service atmosphere

Allowed variations:

- Human activity
- Seasonal details
- Time of service
- Camera movement for video
- Subtle lighting refinements
- Export formats
- Crops for different platforms

Forbidden variations:

- New restaurant layout
- Different architectural style
- Different bar geometry
- Different kitchen placement
- Sci-fi elements
- Floating UI
- Android inserted into the master base image
- Logos or text inside the restaurant
